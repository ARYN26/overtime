Four steps. That's it.

How to apply

No tricks, no learning curve. Clean nose, press on, go.

1

Clean and Dry

Wash and thoroughly dry your nose to remove any oil, sweat, or moisture.

2

Peel & Apply

Peel off the backing and firmly apply the strip across the bridge of your nose.

3

Hold

Press and hold the strip in place for 10 seconds to ensure a secure bond.

4

Feel the Difference

Enjoy easier breathing and improved airflow, starting in seconds.

Pro tip

Apply 5 minutes before warm-up so the adhesive fully sets — it'll hold through your sweatiest set. One strip per session; peel off slowly after.

The Why

We couldn't find one that worked.
So we built it.

Overtime started because we couldn't find a nasal strip that actually worked for both workouts and sleep.

Some strips would peel off as soon as we started sweating. Others stayed on, but they simply weren't strong enough to noticeably open our nasal passages. Then there were the bulky magnetic options — we wanted something that was simple, comfortable, and actually effective.

So we built our own.

We designed Overtime with flexible spring bands that provide a stronger lift, a sweat-resistant adhesive that stays put during workouts, and a lightweight bamboo charcoal fabric that's comfortable enough to wear all night.

It's the nasal strip we wanted but couldn't find — built to help you breathe better, whether you're training, recovering, or sleeping.
